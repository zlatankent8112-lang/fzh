Setup Instructions
How to get M-PESA Credentials:
  1. Sandbox Testing (Free):
    • Visit Safaricom Developer Portal
    • Create an account and login
    • Create a new app (select "Lipa Na M-PESA Online")
    • Copy Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, and Test Credentials
    • Use sandbox credentials below for testing
  2. Production (Live Payments):
    • Contact Safaricom Business (business@safaricom.co.ke or 0711 222 222)
    • Apply for Lipa Na M-PESA Paybill/Till number
    • Submit KYC documents (CR12, PIN certificate, etc.)
    • Once approved, Safaricom will provide production credentials
    • Update configuration with production credentials
Important: Each school must have their own M-PESA credentials. Transaction fees are charged to the school's M-PESA account, not to you as the software provider.
